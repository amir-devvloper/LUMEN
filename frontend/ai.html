How the insight is built

  1. 1

    Only finished sessions count

    Sessions you abandoned, and anything under a minute, are left out so a stray click can't move the numbers.

  2. 2

    Focused time, not wall-clock time

    Each session contributes the part of its block you actually ran: its length minus whatever was left on the clock.

  3. 3

    Grouped by local hour

    Start times are stored in UTC and converted to your server's local clock, so the "best window" lines up with your day.

  4. 4

    The busiest hour wins

    The hour block with the most completed sessions over 28 days becomes your window; ties break toward the earlier hour.

Rule-based, for now

No model is called. The text comes from generateInsight() in backend/services/openai.service.js — the one place to swap in a real completion. Callers only ever see { title, body }, so nothing else has to change.
